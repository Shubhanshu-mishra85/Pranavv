🔐 Privacy & Responsible Technology

Your trust is important.

PRANAV is designed with privacy, verification, accessibility and responsible healthcare technology at the centre of the experience.

Prototype Privacy & Safety Information
🔒

Privacy First

Sensitive donor and healthcare information should be handled carefully and should not be exposed through public listings.

🛡️

Verification First

Potential resource information is clearly separated from verified availability.

🤖

Responsible AI

AI supports navigation and information workflows without independently diagnosing or prescribing.

01

Information PRANAV may handle

Depending on the feature being used, the PRANAV prototype may ask users to enter information needed to support a healthcare coordination workflow.

  • Emergency request information such as blood group, component, urgency and location.
  • Healthcare facility information provided by the user.
  • Contact information when required for coordination.
  • Donor profile information when a donor voluntarily registers through the platform.
  • Information entered into prototype prescription and medicine-review features.
02

Donor privacy

PRANAV is designed around a privacy-first donor coordination approach. Donor information should not be displayed as a publicly searchable personal directory.

Any real-world donor eligibility, suitability, donation procedure and final decision must be handled by an authorised blood centre and qualified professionals.

03

Verification and availability

A resource discovered through PRANAV is not automatically a confirmed blood unit or guaranteed availability.

  • Potential Match means a resource signal based on available information.
  • Verified Availability requires confirmation from the appropriate authorised facility.
  • Availability information can change over time.
  • Users should confirm directly with the relevant facility before relying on resource information.
04

PRANAV AI

PRANAV AI is designed as an assistance layer for navigation, education, accessibility and healthcare coordination.

  • It can help users understand available platform workflows.
  • It can provide simple-language explanations.
  • It can help users navigate emergency request and tracking features.
  • It should not independently diagnose medical conditions.
  • It should not independently prescribe medicines or calculate personalised medication doses.
05

Prescription & medicine information

The prescription intelligence feature is intended to organise visible information from a prescription or medicine package, such as medicine name, strength, manufacturing date, expiry date, batch number and instructions written by the prescriber.

OCR or AI extraction may contain errors. Users should compare extracted information with the original prescription and packaging.

06

How information is used

In a real deployment, information should only be processed for clearly defined purposes connected to the requested healthcare coordination service and according to applicable privacy and healthcare requirements.

  • Supporting emergency request coordination.
  • Supporting resource discovery and verification workflows.
  • Supporting request status and communication.
  • Improving accessibility and user experience where permitted.
07

Security approach

A production version of PRANAV should use appropriate authentication, authorisation, encryption, secure storage, access controls, logging and data-retention practices.

The current website is a prototype and should not be treated as a production healthcare-record system.

⚠️ Healthcare safety

PRANAV does not collect, test, store, transport or release blood. Actual blood handling, compatibility decisions, release and transfusion remain with authorised blood centres and healthcare professionals.

💊 Medication safety

PRANAV does not calculate age-wise medicine doses. If a prescription is unclear, incomplete or appears inconsistent, the user should confirm it with the prescribing doctor or a qualified pharmacist rather than guessing.
