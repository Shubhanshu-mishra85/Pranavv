🔐

Privacy by design

Donor names and contact details should never appear as a public searchable list. PRANAV is designed around controlled coordination rather than public exposure of personal information.

🧠

Intelligent coordination

Potential matching can consider blood group, location, availability information and request context. A potential match is never treated as a guaranteed donor or clinical decision.
