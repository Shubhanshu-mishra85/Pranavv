🛡️ Safety Check

A simple checklist to help verify prescription and medicine information before use.

✓
Medicine Name Confirm that the medicine name matches the original prescription and packaging.
✓
Strength Check the strength printed on the medicine package against the prescription.
✓
Expiry Date Do not rely on an unclear or unreadable date. Verify the original packaging.
✓
Prescription Instructions Follow the instructions written by the prescribing healthcare professional.
✓
Unclear Information Ask the prescribing doctor or pharmacist instead of guessing the medicine or dose.
💊 About Dosage

PRANAV will not independently calculate an age-wise mg dose. The patient's age may be displayed as prescription context, but the actual dosage must come from the authorised prescription and be confirmed by a qualified healthcare professional.
