Important healthcare safeguard

PRANAV must not independently prescribe medicines or determine an age-based dose in place of a doctor. Any dosage information shown by the system should be treated as educational guidance only and verified with the prescribing clinician or a qualified pharmacist, especially for children, older adults and patients with other medical conditions.
