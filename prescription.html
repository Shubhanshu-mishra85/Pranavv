Medication safety

Technology should clarify, not replace your doctor.

PRANAV can help organise visible information, while medication decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

🔎

Read visible information

Extract or organise medicine names, strengths, batch numbers and visible manufacturing or expiry information.

📋

Preserve written instructions

Show the dose or timing instructions exactly as written where they are clearly readable, rather than inventing new instructions.

⚠️

Flag uncertainty

Unclear, missing or potentially expired information should be flagged for verification.

👨‍⚕️

Professional confirmation

Final medicine choice, dose and treatment instructions must come from the prescribing doctor or pharmacist.
